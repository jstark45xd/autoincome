Why Consistent Content Is So Hard

Most business owners know they should post regularly, but the hardest part is often deciding what to post.

You don't need to reinvent your content every day. A simple system can give you enough ideas to educate your audience, build trust, answer questions, and introduce your products or services.

30 Content Ideas

IDEA 1

Introduce the Problem

Explain a problem your customers regularly experience and how your business helps solve it.

IDEA 2

Share a Quick Tip

Give your audience one simple action they can take today to improve a result they care about.

IDEA 3

Answer a FAQ

Answer one question customers frequently ask before purchasing your product or service.

IDEA 4

Behind the Scenes

Show part of your process that customers normally don't get to see.

IDEA 5

Mistake to Avoid

Explain a common mistake people make and what they should do instead.

IDEA 6

Customer Result

Share a customer story, transformation, testimonial, or measurable result when you have permission.

IDEA 7

Ask Your Audience

Ask a simple question about your audience's goals, frustrations, or challenges.

IDEA 8

Common Misconception

Explain something people often believe about your industry and what actually happens.

IDEA 9

Quick How-To

Teach your audience how to complete one useful task related to your product or service.

IDEA 10

Buyer Question

Answer a question someone might have immediately before deciding whether to buy.

IDEA 11

Your Process

Break your process into three to five easy-to-understand steps.

IDEA 12

Lesson Learned

Share something your business has learned through experience.

IDEA 13

Customer Story

Tell a short story about a customer's problem, solution, and outcome.

IDEA 14

Audience Question

Ask your audience what their biggest challenge is with a topic related to your business.

IDEA 15

Compare Two Options

Compare two approaches and explain when each one makes sense.

IDEA 16

Myth vs. Fact

Correct one misconception your ideal customers may have.

IDEA 17

Teach Something

Explain one concept your audience needs to understand to make a better decision.

IDEA 18

Before and After

Show a transformation, improvement, process, or result.

IDEA 19

Useful Resources

Recommend useful tools, resources, websites, books, or processes.

IDEA 20

Share an Opinion

Share a thoughtful opinion about something happening in your industry and explain why.

IDEA 21

Break Down an Example

Take an example your audience can learn from and explain what makes it effective.

IDEA 22

Handle an Objection

Address a genuine reason someone might hesitate before buying.

IDEA 23

Testimonial

Share a real customer testimonial when you have permission to do so.

IDEA 24

Product Walkthrough

Show exactly what customers receive when they purchase your product or service.

IDEA 25

Explain the Value

Explain how your product or service can save customers time, money, or effort.

IDEA 26

Common Buyer Question

Answer one final question that could help someone feel confident taking the next step.

IDEA 27

Make an Offer

Clearly explain what you're offering and what customers receive.

IDEA 28

Who It's For

Explain who your product or service is designed to help.

IDEA 29

Three Things to Remember

Summarize three important lessons your audience can use immediately.

IDEA 30

Clear Call to Action

Tell your audience exactly what you want them to do next.
