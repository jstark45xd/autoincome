30 Social Media Content Ideas for Small Businesses

Running out of things to post? Here are 30 practical social media content ideas you can adapt for almost any small business.

30 ideas you can use today

1. Introduce your business

Explain who you are, what you do, and who you help.

2. Share a quick tip

Give your audience one useful action they can take today.

3. Answer a common question

Turn a question customers frequently ask into a post.

4. Show behind the scenes

Give people a look at how your business works.

5. Explain a common mistake

Help your audience avoid something that commonly causes problems.

6. Share a customer story

Tell the story of how your product or service helped a customer.

7. Ask your audience a question

Ask about their biggest challenge, goal, or preference.

8. Bust a misconception

Explain something people often misunderstand about your industry.

9. Share a useful resource

Recommend a tool, website, book, or resource your audience may find useful.

10. Show your process

Break down how you go from a customer's problem to the finished result.

11. Share a before and after

Show a genuine transformation or improvement.

12. Answer a customer FAQ

Answer a question that helps someone make a buying decision.

13. Share your best advice

Give one lesson you've learned through experience.

14. Compare two approaches

Explain the differences between two common ways of solving a problem.

15. Share a myth vs. fact

Correct a misconception your customers frequently encounter.

16. Explain your pricing

Explain what customers receive and what makes your offer valuable.

17. Share a lesson from a mistake

Talk about something that didn't work and what you learned.

18. Show your workspace

Give your audience a glimpse into where the work happens.

19. Explain something simply

Take a confusing topic and break it into simple steps.

20. Share a testimonial

With permission, share what a real customer said about your business.

21. Recommend three tools

Share three tools or resources that make your audience's work easier.

22. Answer an objection

Address a genuine reason someone might hesitate before buying.

23. Share your opinion

Give a thoughtful opinion about a relevant industry topic.

24. Show what customers receive

Walk through your product or service so people know what to expect.

25. Share a checklist

Give your audience a short checklist they can use immediately.

26. Ask what people need help with

Use your audience's answers to discover future content topics.

27. Share a case study

Explain the customer's problem, your solution, and the result.

28. Explain who your product is for

Clearly describe the type of customer who benefits most from your offer.

29. Summarize your best advice

Combine several useful lessons into one easy-to-save post.

30. Make a clear offer

Tell people what you sell, who it's for, and how they can get it.
