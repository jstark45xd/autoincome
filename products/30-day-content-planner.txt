30-DAY CONTENT PLANNER

30 Days of Social Media Content Without the Daily Guesswork

A practical, ready-to-use content planning workbook for small businesses, entrepreneurs, creators, and service providers.

Use this planner to build a consistent 30-day posting schedule without starting from a blank page every morning.

---

HOW TO USE THIS PLANNER

For each day:

1. Choose the suggested content idea.
2. Adapt the hook to your business.
3. Add your own example, experience, or proof.
4. Finish with the suggested call to action.
5. Choose the platform where your audience is most active.
6. Schedule or publish the post.

The goal isn't to create perfect content.

The goal is to consistently publish useful content that gives your audience a reason to remember, trust, and eventually buy from you.

WEEK 1 — BUILD A FOUNDATION

DAY 1 — INTRODUCE THE PROBLEM

Goal: Help your audience recognize a problem you solve.

Hook:
"Struggling with [problem]? You're not the only one."

Content:
Explain the problem your business helps customers solve. Describe what makes the problem frustrating, expensive, confusing, or time-consuming.

Proof/example:
Share a quick example of how this problem affects a real customer.

Call to action:
"Have you dealt with this? Tell me below."

Best format:
Text post, carousel, or short video.

CONTENT REPURPOSING

You don't always need to create something completely new.

Turn one strong idea into multiple pieces of content:

One idea → five posts

1. Educational post
Teach the idea.

2. Short video
Explain it in 30–60 seconds.

3. Carousel
Break the idea into several steps.

4. Question post
Ask your audience what they think.

5. Promotional post
Connect the problem back to your product or service.

This allows one useful idea to work harder for your business.
